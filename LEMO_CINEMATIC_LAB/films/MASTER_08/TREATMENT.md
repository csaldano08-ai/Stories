# MASTER 08 — *Last Train Home*
Style: **80s Cel Anime** (`cel-anime-80s`) · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · ≈70 s · English · stereo

Written before opening the style's `DEMO.md` or demo code (AGENTS.md, "Treatment first").

## 0. Frame rate decision
The user's spec is 30 fps. 80s OVAs reached viewers on NTSC tape at 29.97, so 30 fps is period-true for a *tape*
look. Drawings step inside that grid: **characters on twos (15 drawings/s) for running and fast action, on threes
(10/s) for acting beats**; camera, rain, light sweeps and flares move on ones (30/s). No 3:2 pulldown judder.

## 1. Three candidate structures
1. **The straight chase** — run → kitten → the train waited → the ride. Clear, linear, the brief as written.
2. **A countdown spine** — the station clock and her wristwatch cut in at 23:57 / 23:58 / 23:59 / 00:00. The clock
   passes departure time *while she is crouched over the kitten* — the audience knows she has missed it before she does.
3. **Reflection bookend** — open on the end image (her reflection with a sleeping kitten on a night window), then
   flash back to the run, return to the window.

**Chosen: 1 with the spine of 2.** The bookend (3) gives away the outcome and kills the only suspense the film has.
The countdown inserts add stakes without dialogue (show, don't tell), and they create the turn: the watch reads
00:00, she looks up expecting an empty track — and the train is still there. The ending is earned by the clock.

## 2. Logline and arc
*A young woman sprinting for the last train stops for a soaked kitten — and finds that someone else stopped for her.*

- **Setup** (0–15 s): late, raining, sprinting; the last train is at the platform, the clock says she will not make it.
- **Turn** (15–37 s): a mew. She stops. Her choice costs her the train — the clock passes 23:59 while she wraps the
  kitten in her own jacket.
- **Ending** (37–70 s): the train waited; the driver saw her. Warmth replaces rain: inside the car the kitten sleeps,
  the city slides by, the rain stops.

Theme carried by colour: cold blue rain → her **vermilion jacket** (the one accent colour, STYLE §3) becomes the
kitten's shelter → the **amber interior** of the train repeats that warmth back to her.

## 3. Benchmark (grammar only)
- *Learn from* late-80s OVA night-city sequences in general and from Makoto Shinkai's early train-and-rain shorts
  as a modern descendant: painted wet-street plates under flat cels, rain on three depths, the held close-up as
  drama, light sweeping across a seated figure on a moving train, sound design that drops music for rain.
- *Learn from* 1980s city-pop ballads: FM electric piano, fretless/slap bass, saxophone lead, gated snare.
- *Do not take*: any character design, any specific shot, any melody, logo, line name, station name or sign text.
  All signs use generic words (CAFE, 24H, HOTEL, BAR, PHARMACY). The train livery and line are invented.

## 4. Characters (full bibles in `bible/`)
- **Aki** (protagonist, 24, junior draughtsperson, leaves the office at 23:52). 7.5 heads tall, slim, long almond
  eyes with dark-brown irises, shoulder-length dark-plum hair with a side part and two long locks, vermilion
  short trench jacket over a cream knit, charcoal pencil skirt, black tights, low brown boots, tan shoulder bag.
- **The kitten** (≈8 weeks): grey tabby, white chest and front paws, amber-green eyes, pink nose. Wet = spiky fur.
- **The driver** (late 50s): navy uniform and cap, grey moustache, square face, crow's-feet, white gloves.
  Seen only through/at his cab window.

## 7. Cue map
- Tempo **96 bpm** (beat 0.625 s, bar 2.5 s) throughout, so every section sits on one grid.
- §A Run (0–16 s, bars 1–6.4): drum machine 16ths + gated snare on 2/4, slap bass, chorused guitar chanks, FM EP stabs,
  urgent synth-brass hits on the watch insert and the wide. Departure melody (diegetic, 4-note bell chime ×2) at 9.0.
- **Cut to silence at 16.0 s** (bar 7 downbeat): rain only.
- §B Kitten (18–37.5 s): no drums. FM electric piano, maj7/9 chords, slow string pad; fretless-style bass notes on bar
  downbeats from 23.5. Phrase ends at 36.0 under the clock jump, then **silence #2** (37.5–38.6).
- §C Return (38.6–52 s): one EP chord on the reveal; soft kick + brush-like hats return at 40.0; bass walks; the
  driver/thanks lines sit over a ducked bed.
- §D Ride (52–70 s): full city-pop ballad chorus: sax lead melody (original), EP, bass, gated snare, pad;
  semitone lift at 64.0 for the last phrase; final maj9 chord rings out under the city wide.
- Cuts land on bar lines or half-bars: 3.0? (no: 2.5/3.75) — exact hit list in `timeline.js` → `cuecheck`.

## 9. Subtitles and titles
Burned into the tape inside the CRT pass: Barlow Semi Condensed 600, 54 px, cream-yellow with a thick dark outline,
~96 px above the bottom. Aki = cream; the driver = pale cyan (second-speaker colour, STYLE §4). Hold ≥ max(1.8 s,
speech + 0.6 s). No title card (no repetitive studio intro, no CTA): the film ends on the city.
