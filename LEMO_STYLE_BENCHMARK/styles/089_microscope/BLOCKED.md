# 089 · Microscope — BLOCKED

BLOCKED. STYLE.md §1: "The specimen is a photograph: a real micrograph or whole-slide scan of the actual sample, CC0, public domain or CC BY. Nothing on the specimen is drawn, retouched or generated." Every reading is computed from the file's pixel size.

A native version needs real micrographs (for this story, e.g. a drop of rain water or a fibre of the coat) and the library ships none. The open micrograph sources answer `403 (agent proxy: no rule allows this host)`: `idr.openmicroscopy.org`, `upload.wikimedia.org`, `commons.wikimedia.org`, `images.nasa.gov`. Drawing cells or crystals in code would break the style's first rule, and a micrograph of a sample that is not from the scene would present an invented link as a fact; the style is not replaced by a generic version.

(Network: checked 2026-11-27 from this cloud session.)

What a native build would be, once unblocked: a whole-slide pyramid of a real rain-water sample (or a wool-fibre slide) as the specimen; a log dive from the slide in the hand to a single particle as the splash lands; the focal-plane sweep on the watch beat; the scale bar stepping 1-2-5; the chime as the objective click on the last magnification. Every on-screen number from the file's metadata, labelled as measured on that sample, not on AKI's puddle.
