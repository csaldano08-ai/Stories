BLOCKED: this style is a broadcast news package whose frame is built around a channel sting and a permanent channel bug (STYLE.md §2–3: "the frame is the graphic, the strap, the bug and a source line"), and whose every graphic states one sourced fact ("every number from the source page"; illustration plates "never stand in for the event, the place or the people").
