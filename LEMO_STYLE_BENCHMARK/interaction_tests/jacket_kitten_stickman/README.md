# Interaction test — the jacket and the kitten (stickman on marker paper)

The same 7 s action as `../jacket_kitten_anime/` (same rig poses and the same precomputed cloth record), drawn as a stickman: black marker lines on paper, one red marker for the jacket. `final.mp4` (1920×1080, 30 fps, 210 frames, silent stereo), contact sheet in `qa/contact_sheet.png`.

Every step reads cleanly at this level of abstraction: the folded jacket on the forearm, the gathering step and kneel, the collar taken by the near hand, the jacket falling open, spread in front of her, lowered over the kitten, the ends tucked under it, and the lift to the chest with continuous hand contact. Stick drawing makes the contact points easy to check, so it doubles as a readable debug view of the cloth action.

Weak points (shared with the anime version): the jacket is a strip, not a jacket silhouette; when it slides off the forearm (2.5–2.8 s) it crumples onto the ground before it is lifted; at the end part of it hangs below the bundle.
