# Interaction test — the jacket and the kitten (cartoon)

The same 7 s action as `../jacket_kitten_anime/` (same rig poses, same precomputed cloth record) in a flat cartoon treatment: bold black outlines,
flat colours, rounded tube limbs, a big round head, our kitten with big eyes, a flat night street under a moon. No squash or stretch:
the tubes follow the rig, so every limb keeps its length. `final.mp4` (1920×1080, 30 fps, 210 frames, silent stereo); contact sheet in `qa/contact_sheet.png`.

The action reads clearly (take, open, spread, lay over, tuck, lift). Weak points are the same as the other versions: the jacket is a strip
(a bold red tube) rather than a jacket shape, and part of it hangs below the bundle at the end.
